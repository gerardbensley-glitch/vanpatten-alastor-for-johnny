THE HOUSE · VAN PATTEN-ALASTOR

A House of Personal Standard.

Van Patten-Alastor is a private house devoted to the considered art of living. Bringing together personal presentation, fine objects, lifestyle direction and private-client services, we provide a discreet and individual approach to the arrangements that shape one's way of life.

Our work is guided by an enduring appreciation for quality, proportion, authenticity and distinction.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

The art of living, considered as a whole.

A distinguished way of life is seldom defined by any single possession. It is found in the harmony between one's appearance, surroundings, interests and daily pursuits.

At Van Patten-Alastor, we regard these elements as parts of a greater whole. Our purpose is to help each client establish a personal standard that reflects their character, circumstances and aspirations.

Whether concerning a wardrobe, a professional appearance, a residence, an automobile or the finer details of everyday life, each undertaking receives the same attention to suitability, quality and detail.

We believe in considered selection rather than excess, lasting quality rather than passing fashion, and sound judgement above all.

Authenticity, responsible sourcing and transparency remain fundamental to every recommendation we make.

OUR APPROACH

Personal service, without compromise.

Every client and every undertaking is different. Our work therefore begins with an understanding of the individual, followed by a carefully considered course of action.

01

Discovery

Understanding the individual.

We begin by establishing an understanding of the client's preferences, circumstances, interests, aspirations and requirements. No recommendation is made without first understanding the person for whom it is intended.

02

Direction

Establishing a standard.

Drawing upon the information gathered, we develop a coherent direction. This may include personal style, wardrobe planning, selected acquisitions, lifestyle arrangements and the priorities of a wider brief.

03

Arrangement

Bringing the details together.

With the direction established, we undertake the coordination of selected goods, sourcing, independent specialists and other arrangements. All proposals are presented for the client's consideration and approval.

04

Continuity

Maintaining the standard.

Where requested, our relationship extends beyond the initial undertaking. We remain available to review arrangements, refine selections and assist with future requirements as circumstances evolve.

OUR PRINCIPLE

Distinction is never imposed.
It is cultivated.

We hold that good taste cannot be prescribed, nor can distinction be measured by the accumulation of possessions. It is a matter of discernment, individuality and the appreciation of things well considered.

Van Patten-Alastor does not seek to define how a client ought to live. Rather, we provide the attention, direction and assistance through which each individual may establish a standard of their own.

Quality in selection. Discretion in service. Judgement in every detail.
