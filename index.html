THE HOUSE · OUR PHILOSOPHY

Build the whole picture.

Van Patten-Alastor is a private lifestyle and image consultancy based in Miami. We help clients define the character they wish to present, then coordinate the details that make that identity tangible.

From a considered wardrobe and a personal watch collection to a residence, professional environment, travel calendar and public image, every element should feel connected. The work begins with the individual: their ambitions, practical needs, interests and budget.
