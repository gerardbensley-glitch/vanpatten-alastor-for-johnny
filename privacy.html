Privacy notice.

Last updated: January 2026

Introduction

Van Patten-Alastor is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients and website visitors. This notice explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information in connection with our services and this website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information when you interact with us:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, telephone number, and mailing address provided through our enquiry form or in direct correspondence.
  • Professional information: Professional title, industry, company name, and related details relevant to our services.
  • Preferences and interests: Style preferences, lifestyle interests, budget ranges, and project requirements discussed during consultations.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage data collected automatically through standard web technologies.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide consulting services, product sourcing, and lifestyle coordination as requested.
  • To communicate with you regarding consultations, proposals, and project updates.
  • To understand your preferences and tailor our recommendations accordingly.
  • To maintain our client relationships and provide ongoing support where agreed.
  • To improve our services and website experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our legitimate business interests.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Independent specialists and partners: When necessary to fulfil your requests, such as retailers, tailors, photographers, or other service providers, and only to the extent required for the specific project.
  • Professional advisors: Legal, financial, or other advisors as necessary for legitimate business purposes.
  • Service providers: Technology and administrative service providers who assist in operating our business, under strict confidentiality agreements.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and maintain our business relationships, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and business requirements.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

International Data Transfers

Van Patten-Alastor operates in multiple jurisdictions. Information may be transferred and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Website Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality and analyze usage patterns. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. We will notify clients of material changes by email or through other reasonable means. The updated notice will be effective immediately upon posting.
