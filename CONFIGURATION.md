# Version 3.0 configuration checklist

## Before public launch
- [ ] Confirm official legal business name, address and contact email.
- [ ] Configure secure enquiry endpoint or CRM.
- [ ] Review privacy notice and terms with Florida counsel.
- [ ] Confirm supplier accounts, authorization and product provenance.
- [ ] Recheck every brand price and product URL immediately before launch.
- [ ] Set actual Van Patten-Alastor retail pricing and service-fee policy.
- [ ] Confirm product availability, shipping, warranties and returns.
- [ ] Confirm photo licensing and replace any unlicensed or hotlinked images.
- [ ] Test desktop/mobile, keyboard navigation, screen readers and image failures.
- [ ] Add final domain, canonical URLs, sitemap and analytics only after consent/legal review.

## Local preview
Run `python -m http.server 8000` from this folder and open `http://localhost:8000`.
